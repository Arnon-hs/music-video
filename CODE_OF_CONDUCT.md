# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome contributors with different experience levels, communicate decisions in the relevant public issue or pull request, and remove private data from examples and logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or pressuring maintainers or contributors for unpaid work are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently block participants when necessary to protect the community.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the maintainer through the private contact method listed on the [Arnon-hs GitHub profile](https://github.com/Arnon-hs). Do not publish private evidence in an issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical, but this volunteer project provides no response-time guarantee.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
